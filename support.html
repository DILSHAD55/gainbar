GainBar Support

Getting started

Open GainBar, click Turn On GainBar and allow the "record system audio" prompt. Then click the three-slider icon in the menu bar.

No sound reaches GainBar / apps go silent

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, make sure GainBar is allowed, then turn GainBar off and on. Turning GainBar off always restores normal sound.

An app doesn't appear

Apps appear while they play sound. Start playback, then open the mixer.

Auto-Duck

Expand an app's row (click its icon) and tick "Lower other apps when this plays" to make it count as a call app.

Requirements

macOS 14.4 or later.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/DILSHAD55/gainbar/issues and we will get back to you.
