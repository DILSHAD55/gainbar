GainBar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

GainBar does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

System audio

With your permission, GainBar uses Apple's Core Audio process taps to change the volume of each app and apply your equalizer while the sound plays. Audio is processed live in memory. It is never recorded, saved to disk or sent anywhere.

Microphone

The microphone is used only during headphone balance calibration, which you start yourself. The sound is analysed in memory to compare left and right loudness and is then discarded.

Network

The optional headphone-correction browser downloads public profile files from the open AutoEq project on GitHub when you open it. Nothing about you or your device is uploaded.

Settings

Your mixer and equalizer settings are stored only on your Mac.
